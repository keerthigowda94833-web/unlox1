lightbulb Daily Eco-Habits

Invest in a set of reusable mesh bags for fruits and vegetables. They are washable, durable, and significantly cut down on single-use plastic waste during your grocery runs. Keep them stored inside your main reusable shopping bags so you never forget them.

fact_check Quick Sort Guide

check_circle Always Do

  • • Flatten cardboard boxes completely.
  • • Keep paper dry and clean.
  • • Check local numbers for plastics.

cancel Never Do

  • • "Wish-cycle" unknown items.
  • • Put plastic bags in kerbside bins.
  • • Recycle greasy pizza boxes.
