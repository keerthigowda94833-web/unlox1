Mastering Waste Classification

Understanding the different categories of waste is the first step towards an effective environmental stewardship. Explore the types below.

compost

Biodegradable

Organic matter that can be broken down by microorganisms into simpler, stable compounds. Proper disposal reduces landfill methane emissions.

Examples

Food Scraps Yard Waste Coffee Grounds Paper Towels (unsoiled)
recycling

Recyclable

Materials that can be processed and reformed into new products. Ensuring these are clean and dry is crucial for effective recycling.

Examples

Paper & Cardboard Glass Bottles Metal Cans Rigid Plastics
devices

Electronic

Discarded electrical or electronic devices. Contains valuable materials like gold and copper, but also toxic substances like lead and mercury.

Examples

Old Phones Computers Cables
warning

Hazardous

Waste with properties that make it dangerous or capable of having a harmful effect on human health or the environment. Requires specialized disposal facilities.

Examples

Batteries Paint Cans Cleaning Chemicals
