Learn About Waste Categories

Waste segregation helps protect the environment. Learn about the four major waste categories below.

Recyclable

Materials that can be reused

Recyclable waste includes paper, cardboard, glass bottles, plastic bottles, and metal cans.

Examples: Newspaper, Cardboard, Plastic Bottle, Glass Bottle.
Biodegradable

Organic Waste

Biodegradable waste decomposes naturally and can be converted into compost.

Examples: Banana Peel, Leaves, Food Waste, Tea Bag.
Electronic

E-Waste

Electronic waste includes old electronic devices that should be recycled safely.

Examples: Mobile Phone, Laptop, Charger, Cable.
Hazardous

Hazardous Waste

Hazardous waste contains harmful chemicals that require special disposal.

Examples: Batteries, Paint, Chemicals, Medicines.
