Waste Disposal Tips

Follow these simple tips to dispose of waste correctly and help protect the environment.

♻ Recyclable Waste

Wash plastic bottles and containers before recycling. Flatten cardboard boxes to save space.

🌿 Biodegradable Waste

Put fruit peels, vegetables, leaves, and food waste into a compost bin.

💻 Electronic Waste

Take old mobiles, laptops, chargers, and cables to an authorized e-waste collection center.

⚠ Hazardous Waste

Never throw batteries, chemicals, paints, or medicines into normal dustbins.

✅ Recycling Do's
  • Separate dry and wet waste.
  • Reuse paper and plastic whenever possible.
  • Use reusable shopping bags.
  • Recycle glass and metal items.
❌ Recycling Don'ts
  • Do not mix food waste with recyclable waste.
  • Do not burn plastic.
  • Do not throw batteries into household bins.
  • Do not dump chemicals into drains.
🌍 Save Our Environment

Small actions make a big difference. Segregating waste properly helps reduce pollution, conserve resources, and create a cleaner future.
