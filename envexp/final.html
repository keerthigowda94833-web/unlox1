Why sorting waste matters

Learn how to separate trash into recyclable, biodegradable, electronic, and hazardous waste.

Improper disposal harms our planet, pollutes water, and increases landfill waste. This guide makes waste segregation simple through clear examples, interactive sorting, and a fun quiz.

What happens when waste is sorted?

Recycling saves resources, composting reduces methane, electronics are safely reused, and hazardous materials avoid dangerous contamination.

Fast facts

  • Recycling cuts resource use by up to 70%.
  • Biodegradable waste turns into compost.
  • Electronics contain reusable metals.
  • Hazardous items need special collection.
