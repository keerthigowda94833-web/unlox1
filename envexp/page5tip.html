Quick disposal tips

Recyclable

Rinse containers and remove labels when possible. Do not mix greasy pizza boxes with clean paper.

Biodegradable

Keep food waste separated and avoid plastic bags in compost. Chop large items to help them break down faster.

Electronic

Drop off old electronics and chargers at an e-waste center. Never throw them in the regular bin.

Hazardous

Store chemicals and batteries safely until you can take them to a hazardous waste collection event.

Local rule reminder

Check your local service for pickup days and rules. Many communities require separate bins for recyclables and compost.
