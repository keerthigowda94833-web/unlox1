Learn the four waste categories

Recyclable

What belongs in blue?

Items that can be reused or turned into new products.

  • Paper
  • Plastic bottles
  • Glass jars
  • Metal cans
Biodegradable

What belongs in green?

Items that break down safely in compost or soil.

  • Food scraps
  • Leaves
  • Paper towels
  • Wooden utensils
Electronic

What belongs in orange?

Devices and gadgets that require safe recycling.

  • Old phones
  • Chargers
  • Battery packs
  • Small appliances
Hazardous

What belongs in red?

Materials that can harm people, animals, or the environment.

  • Paint cans
  • Batteries
  • Needles
  • Cleaning chemicals
