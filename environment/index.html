Why Waste Segregation Matters?

Waste segregation means separating waste into different categories before disposal. It helps reduce pollution, improves recycling, saves natural resources, and keeps our surroundings clean and healthy.

Protect Environment

Reduces pollution and keeps nature clean.

Increase Recycling

Reusable materials are recycled efficiently.

Save Resources

Conserves water, energy, and raw materials.

Less Landfill Waste

Reduces the amount of waste dumped in landfills.
